## Description:
Node.js version: V24.21.0

In our web application you can log in and make an account. When logged in you can make a quiz, take quiz, look at your own history, see what quizzes you have taken and the score you got on the quiz.   

As a user you can change your username (changing username does not require new password), change your password, and also delete your whole user. 
That aslo includes deleting your history, as well as deleting all the quizzes you have made. This completes the crud requirments, where you can create a user, read user details, update user information, and delete your own user. 

The web aplication is dynamic, where you can see newly added quizzes and taken quizzes with scores. You can also create new users, quizzes and questions.
